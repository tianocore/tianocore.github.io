TianoCore is a group of open source projects related to [UEFI] and
the Intel&reg; Platform Innovation Framework for UEFI (AKA Tiano).

If you are interested in being involved with our community, we suggest:

* Downloading and using our open source code
  * Look over our [Projects]({{wiki}}/Projects/additional_projects.html) section
  * For [EDK II] projects, checkout the
    [Github Quick Start]({{wiki}}/development/contribution-guides/sourceforge_to_github_quick_start.html) and
    [Getting Started for Developers]({{wiki}}/development/tutorials-howto/getting_started_with_edk_ii.html)
    pages
* Join the [Mailing Lists]({{wiki}}/community/communications/mailing_lists.html) for one or more of
  our projects
* Review the [Reporting Issues]({{wiki}}/community/support-onboarding/reporting_issues.html) and
  [Reporting Security Issues]({{wiki}}/security/processes/reporting_security_issues.html) pages.

You may be required to create an account to participate in certain
areas of our community.

The [GitHub Help](https://help.github.com/) page provides step-by-step
instructions for user registration and basic features supported by
GitHub.

## Add-in Hardware Developers

* See [UEFI Driver Developer Resources with EDK
  II]({{wiki}}/development/tutorials-howto/driver_developer.html) Support UEFI driver development by
  independent hardware vendors (IHV), including the UEFI Driver Wizard
  and UEFI Driver Writers Guide (DWG).

* Both [EDK]({{wiki}}/archives/reference/edk.html) and [EDK II] contain a Developer’s UEFI
  Emulation ([DuetPkg]({{wiki}}/platforms-packages/core-packages/duet_pkg.html)) environment to aid in
  development of UEFI drivers for add-in hardware. One of the
  limitations of the [NT32]({{wiki}}/archives/platforms-packages/nt32.html) emulation environment is
  the inability to communicate directly with hardware. This is of
  particular concern to add-in hardware developers (IHVs), who are
  developing UEFI drivers for their hardware devices.
